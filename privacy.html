Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Who we are

Noiré Skincare (“Noiré”, “we”, “us”) operates this website. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you browse our store, create an account, place an order, or contact us.

Information we collect

We collect the information you give us and the information created when you use the site:

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, and the skin type you add to your profile.
  • Order details: the items you buy, order totals, delivery details, order status, and any return or refund requests.
  • Reviews and activity: product ratings and comments, wishlist and cart activity, and actions you take while signed in.
  • Messages: anything you send through our contact form or chatbot.
  • Marketing preference: whether you chose to receive promotional emails.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser shares, and preferences saved on your device.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process, deliver, and update your orders, and handle returns and refunds.
  • Send order status emails.
  • Answer your questions and give support.
  • Suggest products that suit your skin type.
  • Send promotional emails, only if you opted in.
  • Keep the store secure and prevent misuse.
  • Improve our products and website.

Emails and marketing

Order and account emails are service messages and are sent as needed. We send promotional emails only to customers who opted in. You can change this choice at any time from your account page or by contacting us.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run Noiré, and only as needed:

  • Google Firebase, for sign-in and for storing account, order, and store data.
  • Email delivery services, to send order updates and promotions.
  • Chatbase, which powers our website chatbot.
  • Delivery partners, to fulfil your orders.
  • Authorities, when we are required to by law.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s storage and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your cart, and make pages load faster. Some third-party content on our site, such as fonts and the chatbot, may set its own cookies or collect technical data under its own policies.

How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including legal, accounting, and reporting requirements. Order records may be kept longer for those reasons. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Keeping your information safe

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. Access to our admin tools is limited to authorized staff, and administrator activity is logged. No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used.
  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Object to certain uses of your data.
  • Ask us to erase or block your data.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a common format.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children’s privacy

Our site is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “last updated” date at the top of this page.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or want to use your rights, reach out:
